RESEARCH

Research

Three research directions connect precise perception, reliable connectivity and trusted collaboration in complex and extreme environments.

01PNLPositioning &
Localization

High-Precision Localization and Intelligent Perception in GNSS-Denied Environments

We study the theory, enabling technologies and system methods for high-precision localization, navigation, environmental perception and intelligent inspection under GNSS denial, severe blockage and strong interference. Key problems include positioning accuracy, continuity, environmental understanding and multisource fusion.

Localization · precision perception · multisource fusion · GNSS denial · underground spaces · intelligent inspection

02IoT-NGInternet of Things
Next Generation

Robust Communications and Reliable Networking in Extreme Environments

We investigate reliable communications, robust transmission and cooperative networking under severe physical constraints, including underground, mining, underwater, ice-covered and electromagnetically challenging environments.

Cross-media communication · through-the-earth communication · extreme environments · robust transmission · cooperative networking · reliable connectivity

03AIBIArtificial Intelligence &
Blockchain Intelligence

Intelligent Processing and Trusted Collaboration under Weak Connectivity and Low Trust

We study efficient information processing, trustworthy information flow and collaborative mechanisms for resource-constrained distributed systems. Our work explores the integration of artificial intelligence, reinforcement learning, federated learning, complex networks and blockchain.

Artificial intelligence · AI agents · reinforcement learning · federated learning · complex networks · blockchain · privacy · trusted collaboration
