ABOUT

About

PAI Research Center advances integrated sensing, communication, computing, and intelligence for complex and extreme environments.

Who We Are

PAI Research Center brings together faculty, researchers, engineers and students from academia and industry. The team spans theory, enabling technologies, systems, platforms and research translation.

What We Study

Our work focuses on integrated sensing, communication, computing, and intelligence across three research directions: high-precision localization and intelligent perception in GNSS-denied environments; robust communications and reliable networking in extreme environments; and intelligent processing and trusted collaboration under weak connectivity and low trust.

Problems We Address

We study localization and environmental perception where satellite positioning is unavailable; precision detection and inspection in underground spaces; reliable communications through underground, mining, underwater and other challenging media; and efficient, trustworthy collaboration in resource-constrained distributed systems.
