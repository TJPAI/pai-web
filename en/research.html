Research

Three research directions connect perception, reliable connectivity and trusted intelligence in complex and extreme environments.

01PNLPositioning &
Localization

High-Precision Localization and Intelligent Perception in Complex Environments

We study fundamental theories, key technologies and system-level approaches for high-precision localization, navigation, environmental perception and intelligent inspection in GNSS-denied environments with severe occlusion or interference. Key challenges include positioning accuracy, continuous availability, environmental understanding and multi-source sensor fusion. Applications include underground spaces, utility networks and tunnels, complex buildings, robotics and unmanned systems.

Research ChallengesGNSS denial · severe occlusion · interference · continuous availability

Enabling Technologieslocalization and navigation · precision perception · multi-source fusion · environmental understanding · intelligent inspection

Application Scenariosunderground spaces · utility networks and tunnels · complex buildings · robotics and unmanned systems

02IoT-NGInternet of Things
Next Generation

Robust Communications and Reliable Networking in Complex Environments

We investigate reliable communications, robust transmission and cooperative networking across media and under severe physical constraints, including underground, mining, underwater, ice-covered and electromagnetically challenging environments. We address fragile links, limited bandwidth, complex propagation and system reliability to support resilient connectivity and coordinated operations in complex environments.

Research Challengesfragile links · limited bandwidth · complex propagation · system reliability

Enabling Technologiescross-media communication · through-the-earth communication · robust transmission · cooperative networking · reliable connectivity

Application Scenariosunderground · mining · underwater · ice-covered · electromagnetically challenging environments

03AIBIArtificial Intelligence &
Blockchain Intelligence

Intelligent Information Processing and Trusted Collaboration in Complex Environments

We study efficient information processing, trusted information exchange and collaborative mechanisms for resource-constrained distributed systems with weak connectivity and limited mutual trust. Our work explores how artificial intelligence, reinforcement learning, federated learning, complex networks and blockchain can be integrated in complex systems to improve information efficiency, system robustness, trustworthiness and multi-node collaboration.

Research Challengesresource constraints · weak connectivity · limited trust · information efficiency · multi-node collaboration

Enabling Technologiesartificial intelligence · AI agents · reinforcement learning · federated learning · complex networks · blockchain

Application Scenariosdistributed intelligence · trusted information exchange · multi-node collaboration · complex systems
