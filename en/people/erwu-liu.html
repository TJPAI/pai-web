Erwu Liu

Professor · Director

Erwu Liu

Professor, Tongji University · IET Fellow

Research

Wireless communications and IoT, localization and sensing, integrated sensing and communication, federated and reinforcement learning, complex networks, blockchain and trusted intelligence.

Profile

Professor at Tongji University and Director of PAI Research Center. His work spans complex-environment localization and sensing, robust communications and trusted intelligent systems. He previously worked at Alcatel-Lucent Bell Labs and Imperial College London.

Selected Activities

  • Chief architect of localization and navigation systems for the China International Import Expo
  • Champion of the Microsoft Indoor Localization Competition in 2016 and runner-up in 2018
  • Co-author of Magnetic Communications: Theory and Techniques, published by Cambridge University Press
  • Founding Editor-in-Chief of IET Blockchain
