Challenges

Problems We Address

For complex and extreme environments spanning underground spaces, tunnels, low-altitude airspace, and space–air–ground integrated scenarios, we develop capabilities in precise sensing, reliable connectivity, and trusted collaboration.

01

High-precision localization and perception in complex environments

We improve the accuracy and continuity of localization, navigation and environmental perception where satellite positioning is unavailable or affected by severe occlusion and strong interference.

Localization · continuity · multi-source sensing
02

Precision detection and intelligent inspection in underground spaces

We study precision detection, intelligent inspection and complex-environment understanding for underground utility networks, tunnels and other underground spaces.

Underground · detection · inspection
03

Reliable communication and networking through challenging media

We improve resilient connectivity in challenging propagation conditions, including underground, mining, underwater and ice-covered environments.

Extreme media · robust links · networking
04

Efficient processing and trusted collaboration under weak connectivity and limited mutual trust

We improve information-processing efficiency, system trustworthiness and multi-node collaboration in resource-constrained distributed environments with limited mutual trust.

Weak links · limited trust · trusted collaboration
