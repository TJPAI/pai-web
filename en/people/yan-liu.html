Yan Liu

Assistant Professor

Yan Liu

Assistant Professor, Tongji University · Master's Supervisor

Research

IoT communications, ultra-reliable low-latency communications, random access and machine learning.

Profile

Selected for a Shanghai talent program for overseas researchers and the 2023 Shanghai Sailing Program for young science and technology researchers. She received her PhD from Queen Mary University of London in 2021 and subsequently conducted postdoctoral research there.

Selected Experience & Achievements

  • Published in IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, IEEE Transactions on Communications and IEEE Wireless Communications, and at IEEE ICC and GLOBECOM
  • Led projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, Shanghai funding programs, the UK EPSRC and industry partners
  • Serves on the editorial board of IEEE Internet of Things Magazine and contributes to technical program committees for IEEE ICC, GLOBECOM and VTC
