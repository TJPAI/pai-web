Shuyan Hu

Associate Professor

Shuyan Hu

Associate Professor, Tongji University · Master's Supervisor

Research

Machine learning, optimization, integrated sensing, communication & computing, and space-air-ground integrated networks.

Profile

She received her PhD in science from Fudan University, with joint training at the National University of Singapore. She subsequently worked as a postdoctoral researcher at Fudan University and was selected for Shanghai's Super Postdoctoral incentive program. Her research focuses on machine learning and optimization theory and their applications to wireless communications, integrated sensing, communication and computing, and space-air-ground integrated networks.

Selected Experience & Achievements

  • Led projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation
  • Published in IEEE Transactions journals and other academic journals
  • Served on technical program committees for IEEE GLOBECOM, ICC and VTC
